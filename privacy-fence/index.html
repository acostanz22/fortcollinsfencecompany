Privacy Fence Installation in Fort Collins, CO

A privacy fence is a solid screen for a backyard or patio. In Fort Collins that is usually a rear-yard fence, up to 6 feet, in wood or vinyl. It is not a security system, and it is not the right product for a front yard or for an open pasture that takes a straight-on wind.

When you need a privacy fence

Build privacy when the job is blocking a view, not marking a field.

Shared side and rear lines

The neighbor’s deck, an alley, or a busy side street is the usual reason for a solid 6-foot screen.

Patio rooms

A short run around a patio can do the job without fencing the whole lot.

Wind-aware builds

A solid panel catches gusts. Post depth, spacing, and a shadowbox option are part of the quote on exposed lots.

Not the front

Front-yard height in Fort Collins is generally 4 feet. Privacy belongs behind the front building line.

How the work goes

  1. 1. Decide wood, vinyl, or horizontal metal The screen can be cedar, pine, vinyl, or a horizontal-slat metal panel. Each has a different upkeep and wind story.
  2. 2. Place it in the legal yard Rear, side, alley setback, and any drainage easement. We do not set a privacy wall in an easement because the city can make you move it.
  3. 3. Set posts for a solid sail Posts and depth follow the wind, not a backyard in a sheltered court.
  4. 4. Build the screen and the gate Boards or panels go on after the line is true. The gate is framed so it does not twist the first time someone pulls it in a gust.

What changes the price

We do not publish a per-foot rate. These are the items that move an estimate for this fence.

Height and style

Six-foot solid privacy is more material than a 4-foot screen or a shadowbox of the same length.

Material

Treated pine, cedar, vinyl, and horizontal metal are four different prices.

Wind exposure

An open corner lot may need closer posts than a fenced-in court.

Gates

A wide gate in a solid fence needs a real frame. It is not a panel with hinges.

Neighbors and tear-out

Removing the old fence and dealing with a shared line takes time we include when it is part of the job.

Questions about privacy fence

How much does a 6-foot privacy fence cost?

A level rear yard in treated pine is the baseline. Cedar, vinyl, a slope, gates, and removing the old fence are the items that move it. We do not publish a per-foot rate that pretends those are the same job. Measure the rear and side lines you actually want screened and we will price that.

Can a solid privacy fence handle Front Range wind?

It can, if the posts are set for a solid panel and the spacing is not stretched. A privacy fence is a sail. Shadowbox lets some air through and still blocks most of the view. On a lot with a clear west exposure, we will say if a solid panel is a bad idea.

Do you offer wood and vinyl privacy?

Yes, and horizontal metal slat panels where that look fits the house. Wood can be stained and repaired board by board. Vinyl skips the stain and costs more at the start. The height rules are the same either way.

Do I need the finished side facing my neighbor?

There is no single state rule we can quote as yours. Some HOAs and some neighbor agreements specify the finished face. Structurally we can build board-on-board so both sides look finished. If the HOA packet says which face goes out, send it before we build.

Can you step a privacy fence down a slope?

Yes. Stepping keeps the tops level in sections and is the usual approach for solid panels. The tradeoff is a gap under the low end of each step, which matters if a dog is the reason for the fence. We will show you where those gaps land.
