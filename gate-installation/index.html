Fence Gate Installation and Repair in Fort Collins, CO

A gate fails before the fence does. We build walk gates and drive gates to the opening, the slope, and the weight of the leaf, and we repair hinges, latches, and posts when the rest of the line is still good. The source of most sag is an undersized post, not a bad latch.

When you need a gate, not a new fence

Call about a gate when the opening is the problem, whether the fence is new or old.

New openings

A side-yard walk gate, a backyard mower gate, or a drive gate added to an existing line.

Sagging leaves

The gate drags, the latch misses, or the hinge screws have pulled out of a light post.

Short driveways

If the leaf has nowhere to swing, a sliding gate is the layout to talk about. We do not force a swing into a sidewalk.

Pool and child latches

Self-closing hardware is a specific gate, sized to the rule for that address, not a standard backyard latch.

How the work goes

  1. 1. Measure the clear opening Width, swing direction, slope, and what has to pass: a person, a mower, a truck.
  2. 2. Check the posts If the posts are rotten, heaved, or too light, the new gate goes on new posts. Hanging a heavy leaf on a failed post wastes the gate.
  3. 3. Build or hang the leaf Wood, vinyl, aluminum, steel, or chain-link frames to match the fence that is there.
  4. 4. Set the latch and the swing The gate should close without lifting it. We check it on the slope, not on a level shop floor.

What changes the price

We do not publish a per-foot rate. These are the items that move an estimate for this fence.

Walk versus drive

A 4-foot walk gate and a 12- or 14-foot drive leaf are different steel, posts, and hinges.

Material match

Matching vinyl, cedar, aluminum, or chain link changes the leaf cost.

New posts

Replacing hinge posts, including concrete, is often the real job hiding inside a 'just a gate' call.

Slope

An uphill swing needs clearance. That can mean a different hinge or a shorter leaf.

Hardware

Latches, drop rods, cane bolts, and wheels are specified to the weight, not added as an afterthought.

Questions about gate installation

How much does a gate cost compared with the fence?

On a short fence, the gate can be a large share of the price because of the posts and hardware. On a long privacy run, one walk gate is a smaller share. Drive gates cost more than walk gates on every material. We price the opening you need, including posts if the old ones cannot carry it.

Can you replace a sagging gate without rebuilding the fence?

Yes, when the fence posts on either side of the opening are still sound, or when we replace only those posts. If the whole line is leaning, a new gate on old posts will sag again. We will tell you which of those you have.

Do you install walk gates and drive gates?

Yes. Walk gates for side yards and backyards. Single and double swing drive gates where there is room to swing. Sliding gates when the drive is too short or the swing would block a sidewalk or a parked car. The layout comes before the hardware.

Do I need a sliding gate?

You need one when a swing leaf would hit a slope, a sidewalk, or a vehicle that has to park in the swing path. If there is flat room to swing off the drive, a swing gate is usually simpler. We will say which one fits the opening instead of selling the more complicated leaf.

Can you fix a latch or a dragging gate?

Often that is a hinge, a latch adjustment, or a post that has moved. A drag on day one is usually installation. A drag after a winter is often frost heave. Both are repairable. If the frame itself is twisted, the leaf gets rebuilt.
