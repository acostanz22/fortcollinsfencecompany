Commercial Fence Installation in Fort Collins, CO

Commercial fencing is a spec, not a taller version of a backyard panel. Lots, storage yards, and job sites need a gauge, a height, and a vehicle gate that match the traffic. In Fort Collins, barbed wire is limited to employment and industrial zoning, so a residential detail does not get copied onto a business.

When the job is commercial

Use a commercial spec when people are not the only thing passing the fence.

Lots and yards

Chain link or metal around a yard that has to stay closed after hours, with a gate a truck can use.

Heavier fabric

Terminal posts, brace rails, and a gauge meant for the span. Retail and light industrial are not the same drawing.

Vehicle gates

Swing or slide, sized to the truck, with room to open without blocking the drive aisle.

Zoning limits

Barbed wire and electric fence are not available on every Fort Collins parcel. We check the zone before that detail goes on a quote.

How the work goes

  1. 1. Read the use What is stored, what vehicles enter, and whether the fence is a boundary or a security line. Those pick the spec.
  2. 2. Confirm the parcel rules City, town, or county, plus any site-plan note about height and barbed wire.
  3. 3. Install terminals first Ends, corners, and gate posts are set and braced before fabric or panels go on.
  4. 4. Hang gates and leave the site usable Gates are hung so the business can open them. We do not leave a leaf that has to be lifted by two people.

What changes the price

We do not publish a per-foot rate. These are the items that move an estimate for this fence.

Spec, not style

Gauge, height, coating, and barbed-wire arms where they are allowed move the price more than color.

Linear footage and corners

Commercial yards are longer, and every corner is a braced terminal.

Vehicle gates

A cantilever or a heavy swing gate can cost as much as a long run of fabric.

Demolition

Taking down an old commercial line, including concrete, is its own scope.

Access and schedule

Working around an open business takes more coordination than a backyard on a Tuesday.

Questions about commercial fencing

How much does a commercial fence cost compared with a backyard?

More per foot once the gauge, the terminals, and the vehicle gate are real. A backyard 4-foot chain-link price is not a commercial number. Send the approximate footage, the height you think you need, and what has to drive through. We will quote a spec instead of stretching a residential panel.

Can you install vehicle gates?

Yes. Swing gates where the leaf has room to open off the drive. Sliding gates where it does not. The post and the track or hinge are sized to the leaf and to how often it moves. Tell us the widest truck, including mirrors.

Do you offer chain link and metal for commercial sites?

Yes. Chain link is the usual yard and site fence. Ornamental metal is the street-facing option when the business wants a finished front and chain link on the rest. They can be on the same quote as two different runs.

Do I need barbed wire on a Fort Collins business?

Only some zones allow it. Fort Collins limits barbed wire to employment and industrial districts, and even there it is restricted. A shop in a commercial strip should not assume barbed arms are legal. We check the zoning before we include them.

Can you repair a commercial line while the site stays open?

Often. A damaged bay, a leaning terminal, or a gate that will not close can be repaired in a section. We plan the opening so trucks are not stuck. If the whole line has failed, we will say that a phased replacement is the real plan.

Talk through the line

Tell us the length, the gates, and the town. We will estimate this commercial fencing from the property.
