Picket Fence Installation in Fort Collins, CO

A picket fence is a front-yard boundary, not a privacy screen. In Fort Collins the street side of a house is generally limited to 4 feet, which is why picket, in wood or vinyl, shows up on corners and front walks more than 6-foot privacy does.

When a picket fence is the right job

Use picket when the goal is a clear edge and a finished front, within the height the yard allows.

Front yards

Four feet is the usual Fort Collins front-yard cap. Picket is built for that height. A privacy panel is not.

Defined entries

A short run beside a walk or across a front garden marks the property without hiding the house.

HOA fronts

Some communities allow a picket and reject a solid fence on the street face. The approval packet matters more than the photo you like.

Not a dog run

A 3- or 4-foot picket with gaps will not hold a determined dog. If containment is the job, say so and we will talk about a different fence.

How the work goes

  1. 1. Confirm the yard Front, corner, and the sidewalk setback. A picket too close to a public sidewalk fails the city rule even at the right height.
  2. 2. Pick wood or vinyl Wood can be painted to the trim. Vinyl skips the repaint. Both can be a picket.
  3. 3. Set a straight line Short fences show every wavy post. The line is strung before boards or pickets go on.
  4. 4. Gate at the walk The gate should match the picket spacing and clear the walk, including in snow.

What changes the price

We do not publish a per-foot rate. These are the items that move an estimate for this fence.

Length of the frontage

Picket jobs are often short, so gates and corners are a bigger share of the price than on a long backyard.

Wood versus vinyl

Painted wood costs less in material and more in upkeep. Vinyl costs more up front.

Pickets and caps

Spacing, decorative tops, and a scalloped top take more time than a plain square picket.

Gates

One walk gate on a short fence can be a large part of the quote.

Corner rules

A corner lot has a sight triangle. That can force a lower or more open fence than the rest of the front.

Questions about picket fencing

How much does a front-yard picket fence cost?

Short fronts are not cheap per foot, because the posts, the gate, and the setup are a large part of a 40-foot run. Wood versus vinyl and the number of gates change it more than the picket shape. We measure the frontage, including the walk gate, and quote that.

Can a picket fence be 6 feet tall in a Fort Collins front yard?

Generally, no. The city limits fences in the front yard to 4 feet, and corner sight areas can be lower. A 6-foot privacy fence belongs in the rear yard. If the lot is outside city limits, or an HOA allows less than the city, we follow the stricter rule.

Do you offer wood and vinyl picket?

Yes. Wood picket is painted or stained to the house and will need that finish again. Vinyl picket holds color and does not rot at the sprinkler line. We build both at the height the front yard allows.

Do I need a picket fence to contain a dog?

Usually not. Gaps and a 4-foot height are a poor dog fence for anything that jumps or digs. Picket is for marking a front yard. If the real job is keeping a dog in the back, a privacy or chain-link run with a proper gate is the better quote.

Can you match an existing picket?

Often. Spacing, height, and color are matchable in wood, and sometimes in vinyl if that profile is still sold. A photo and a measurement of one bay tell us whether a match is realistic or whether the old fence should be replaced as one run.

Tell us the length, the gates, and the town. We will estimate this picket fencing from the property.
