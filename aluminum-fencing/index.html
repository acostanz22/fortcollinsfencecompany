Aluminum Fence Installation in Fort Collins, CO

Aluminum is the open fence we use when you want a defined yard without a solid wall. It stays lighter than steel, holds a powder-coated finish through sun and snow, and racks to follow a grade better than a rigid privacy panel.

When aluminum is the right fence

Choose aluminum when the fence has to stay see-through and still look finished from the street.

Front yards

Fort Collins generally limits fences between the house and the street to 4 feet. An open aluminum line meets that height without turning the front into a wall.

Pool and patio edges

A climb-resistant open fence can mark a pool or patio while keeping sightlines. Pool barrier rules depend on the jurisdiction, so we confirm the opening, latch, and height on that lot.

Sloped lots

Rackable aluminum sections follow a grade. Stepped wood panels leave triangular gaps that a dog or a snowdrift will find.

Low upkeep

Aluminum does not need the stain cycle cedar needs, and it does not rust the way bare steel does when snow sits at the bottom rail.

How the work goes

  1. 1. Mark the line and the openings We measure the run, the slope, and every gate before posts go in. A pool gate and a side-yard walk gate are different widths.
  2. 2. Set posts for the grade Posts are set to the soil on that lot, deeper where the line is exposed to wind or where a gate will hang.
  3. 3. Rack the sections Panels are racked to the slope so the bottom rail stays near the ground without a row of wedges.
  4. 4. Hang and latch the gates Hinges, drop rods, and latches are checked so the gate closes without dragging on the high side of the slope.

What changes the price

We do not publish a per-foot rate. These are the items that move an estimate for this fence.

Linear footage and height

A 4-foot front-yard run and a taller side-yard run are priced as different sections of the same job.

Gates

Each opening adds a frame, hardware, and often a heavier post. Drive gates cost more than a 4-foot walk gate.

Slope and corners

More corners, radius work, and steep grade add posts and cutting.

Color and picket style

Standard black ornamental pickets and a custom color or a taller spear top are not the same material order.

Old fence removal

Taking out wood or chain link, hauling it, and resetting the line in the same trench changes the quote.

Questions about aluminum fencing

How much does aluminum fencing cost in Fort Collins?

There is no honest per-foot number until the run is measured. Height, gates, slope, and whether an old fence comes out move the price more than the picket style does. A flat 4-foot front yard is a smaller job than a sloped side yard with a pool gate. Call or send the lot details and we will price that line.

Can you install aluminum on a sloped lot?

Yes. Rackable aluminum is one of the better materials for the grade changes common on older Fort Collins lots and on the benches west of town. The sections tilt with the hill so you are not left with large gaps under a stepped panel. Very steep spots may still need a short transition or an extra post.

Do you offer aluminum that looks like wrought iron?

Yes. Most of the aluminum we install is an ornamental picket style: vertical bars, a top rail, and optional finials. It reads as iron from the curb and does not ask for the paint cycle iron needs after a wet spring. We can keep it simple or add a decorative top if the house calls for it.

Do I need aluminum if the fence is around a pool?

Aluminum is a common pool fence because it stays open and can be built with a self-closing gate. The exact height, latch location, and gap under the bottom rail depend on the code for that address, which is not the same in Fort Collins, Loveland, and unincorporated Larimer County. We size the gate to the rule that applies to the parcel.

Can you repair a bent aluminum rail?

Often, yes. A single bent picket or a loose bracket is a repair. A gate that has racked the whole section, or posts that have heaved out of line, may need that bay replaced. Send a photo of the damaged section and we will say whether it is a part swap or a reset.
