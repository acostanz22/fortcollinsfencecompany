Fence Company in Timnath, CO

Timnath fence jobs are mostly newer, smaller lots. The constraint is usually the HOA spec and the utility or drainage easement, not a long pasture. Fort Collins city fence rules are not the Timnath rule book, even though the towns share a school district and a highway.

What fencing looks like here

Bring the plot and the HOA fence page if you have them. The useful details are easement lines, the required color, and where the gate has to land. History of the town does not change the post layout.

  • Design guidelines often name the height, color, and whether the street side can be fenced at all.
  • Drainage and utility easements run along many rear and side lines. A fence in an easement can be removed. We keep the line out of them.
  • Lot widths are tighter than older Fort Collins blocks, so gate swing and post locations have to be planned against the side yard, not assumed.
  • Vinyl privacy and stained wood are the common matches because communities specify a look.
  • A farm fence is the wrong default here unless the parcel is actually still acreage on the edge.

Questions about fencing in Timnath

Do you follow Timnath HOA fence rules?

Yes. The community spec is part of the quote. If the guideline says a certain vinyl color or a maximum height below what a neighbor built years ago, we follow the guideline. We would rather wait on approval than rebuild a noncompliant line.

Can you keep a fence out of a drainage easement?

Yes. That is a layout task on a lot of Timnath yards. The easement is not spare backyard. We set the fence on the buildable side of it, even if that shortens the yard compared with a fence on the property pin.

Is a Fort Collins permit the right permit for Timnath?

No. Timnath is its own town. Do not pull Fort Collins guidance and assume it covers a Timnath address. We confirm the jurisdiction for the parcel you are fencing.
