Fence Company in Loveland, CO

Loveland fence work splits in two. In-town subdivision lots need backyard privacy, a gate, and whatever height the city and the HOA allow. Lots toward the edges are longer, windier, and sometimes still agricultural. Fort Collins height numbers do not transfer here.

What fencing looks like here

A backyard screen in town and a property line on a larger lot should not be quoted from the same photo. Tell us which one you have. If the house is in an HOA, the approval packet is part of the job, and we would rather see it before panels are ordered.

  • Loveland sets its own fence height and setback rules. We check the parcel instead of using Fort Collins front-yard and rear-yard limits.
  • East and south Loveland have a lot of newer houses where an HOA design review decides color and material before the city rule even matters.
  • Ground changes between the clay benches and the ground closer to the Big Thompson. Post depth is a site decision, not a single number for every Loveland yard.
  • Open lots on the edge take rail, wire, or chain link better than a solid privacy sail.
  • Repairs are common on older wood lines that have lost posts at the ground while the boards still look acceptable from the yard.

Questions about fencing in Loveland

Do Fort Collins fence rules apply in Loveland?

No. Loveland has its own municipal rules, and many neighborhoods add HOA rules that are stricter. A 4-foot front and 6-foot rear is a Fort Collins description, not something to copy onto a Loveland permit question. We confirm the parcel before we commit to a height.

Can you match a Loveland HOA fence?

If the community specifies vinyl color, wood stain, or a certain height, we build to that spec when the product is available. Send the guideline page with the quote request. Guessing from the neighbor’s fence is how people fail architectural review.

What fence makes sense on a larger Loveland lot?

If the lot is still open to the wind, a solid privacy panel around the whole acreage is usually the wrong product. Rail, wire, or chain link covers the long line. Privacy can still wrap the patio. Those are two fences and two prices.
