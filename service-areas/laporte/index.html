Fence Company in Laporte, CO

Laporte is a short drive northwest of Fort Collins and a different kind of lot: smaller houses mixed with acreage, uneven ground, and older wood fences that need a decision between repair and replacement. Deer move through the edges. A straight catalog panel often has to step with the grade.

What fencing looks like here

If you are calling about an old fence, send a photo of the posts at the ground, not only the boards. That photo tells us whether Laporte needs a repair or a new line.

  • Many existing fences are older wood. Sound posts get new boards. Rotted posts get replaced, not covered.
  • Grade changes along the valley mean stepped or racked sections, not one flat run copied from a level backyard.
  • Acreage at the edge of the neighborhood is a rail or wire conversation, separate from a house-yard screen.
  • Laporte is not automatically inside Fort Collins city rules. We confirm the parcel before quoting a height.
  • Gate posts on a slope need extra clearance so the leaf does not dig in at the bottom of the swing.

Questions about fencing in Laporte

Can you repair an older wood fence in Laporte?

Yes, when the posts still hold. Boards, rails, and gates are replaceable. Posts that are soft at the ground or visibly heaved are not worth new boards. We will say which posts stay.

Do you build on uneven ground?

Yes. Wood privacy is usually stepped. Aluminum and some metal can rack. Chain link can follow a mild grade. The material changes how the hill is handled, which is why we look at the slope before we recommend a panel.

Are Laporte lots under Fort Collins fence rules?

Only if the parcel is actually in the city. Many nearby properties are not. Assuming the Fort Collins 4-foot front-yard rule, or ignoring it, are both mistakes until the jurisdiction is confirmed.
