What fencing looks like here

Tell us whether the fence is around the house, around the animals, or both. A 6-foot privacy panel is a house product. The field wants wire or rail and a gate you can drive through.

  • Open ground north of Fort Collins takes a steady wind. Solid panels around a whole field are a poor spec.
  • Horse and cattle fencing needs the right mesh or rail, brace posts, and a gate that clears a trailer.
  • In-town Wellington lots still need privacy and gates, under Wellington’s rules, not Fort Collins alley rules.
  • Runs are longer, so corners and braces are a bigger part of the price than they are on a 60-foot backyard.
  • Repairs on older wire and wood lines are worth doing when the braces are still sound.

Questions about fencing in Wellington

Can you fence pasture near Wellington?

Yes. High-tensile, woven wire, and rail, with end braces and gates sized to the equipment. We need the animal and the longest trailer or truck that uses the opening. A backyard panel quote does not cover that line.

Will Fort Collins barbed-wire rules apply on my Wellington land?

Not automatically. Fort Collins limits barbed wire to certain zones inside that city. A Wellington or county parcel is a different jurisdiction. We still will not put barbed wire on a horse fence. The legal question and the animal question are both real, and they are not the same question.

Can the house have privacy and the field have wire?

Yes. That is the normal split. Privacy or vinyl around the yard, wire or rail on the pasture, and a drive gate between them. We price them as one visit and two fence types so the field is not built like a patio screen.
