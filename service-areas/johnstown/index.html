Fence Company in Johnstown, CO

Johnstown is in Weld County, with new subdivisions on one side of the call and farmland still on the other. Neither one uses Fort Collins fence rules. HOA packets matter in the new neighborhoods. Horse and pasture fencing still matter on the edges.

What fencing looks like here

If the address is a new Johnstown subdivision, send the HOA fence rules. If it is acreage, send the rough length and what has to be kept in. Those two requests should not share a template.

  • Weld County and the Town of Johnstown are not the City of Fort Collins. Height and permit questions start over for each parcel.
  • New neighborhoods commonly specify fence style. We build to the packet.
  • Remaining agricultural ground needs rail or wire and a drive gate, not a vinyl privacy panel stretched down a field.
  • Wind on open Weld County ground is part of the post layout for any solid section near the house.
  • Commercial and shop yards in the area need a chain-link spec, separate from a house quote.

Questions about fencing in Johnstown

Do you install fences in Johnstown HOAs?

Yes. The work is matching the community’s height, material, and color, then setting posts outside any easement. Fort Collins alley diagrams do not describe these lots. The guideline from your association does.

Can you fence farmland in the Johnstown area?

Yes. Wire, rail, and equipment gates for pasture and property lines. Tell us the livestock. A horse fence and a cattle fence are specified differently, and barbed wire is an animal decision as well as a zoning decision.

Is Johnstown covered by Larimer County fence rules?

Johnstown is in Weld County. Some nearby towns straddle a county line. Windsor does. Johnstown does not get Larimer County or Fort Collins rules pasted onto it. We confirm the parcel.
