Fence Company in Berthoud, CO

Berthoud has new subdivision backyards and older parcels that are still open to the wind. A privacy fence behind a new house and a long property line on an agricultural lot are both local jobs. They fail when someone uses one to solve the other. Berthoud is not on the Fort Collins fence code.

What fencing looks like here

Say whether the property is a subdivision lot or an open parcel. That single fact picks the fence faster than a style name does.

  • New neighborhoods often require HOA review of style and color before installation.
  • Open lots south of Loveland take wind. A solid fence around a large field needs a different post plan than a patio screen, and often should not be solid at all.
  • Chain link and wire cover long edges at a lower cost than cedar privacy.
  • In-town backyards still want a gate that fits a mower and a height the town and the HOA both allow.
  • We do not reuse Loveland or Fort Collins setback numbers on a Berthoud address.

Questions about fencing in Berthoud

Can you fence a new Berthoud backyard?

Yes. Privacy in wood or vinyl, plus a gate, is the usual in-town request. If an HOA has to approve the color, that approval comes before we order material. The town’s own height rules still apply on top of the HOA.

What about a longer Berthoud property line?

Chain link, rail, or wire, depending on whether you need to contain animals or only mark the line. A cedar privacy price stretched across several hundred feet of open ground is rarely the right spend, and the wind load is worse.

Do you use Fort Collins permit rules in Berthoud?

No. Berthoud is its own town in Larimer County. We check Berthoud’s requirements for that parcel. Quoting a Fort Collins front-yard height onto a Berthoud lot is a city-name swap, and we do not do it.
