Fence Installation for Fort Collins Lots

Fort Collins lots are a mix of tight older blocks, alley-loaded streets, and newer subdivisions with HOA fence rules. The fence that works depends on which yard it sits in. A front-yard picket, a rear-yard privacy screen, and a commercial line are three different jobs inside the same city.

What fencing looks like here

Older neighborhoods often need a repair or a wood fence that matches what is already on the block. South and east growth areas are more likely to hand you an HOA list of allowed heights and colors before a post can be set. We read the lot first. The City of Fort Collins publishes the height and permit basics in its rules for common projects, and those rules do not automatically apply once you cross into Loveland, Windsor, or unincorporated Larimer County.

  • Inside the city, fences 6 feet and under generally do not need a building permit. Fences taller than 6 feet do, and a fence next to a public right-of-way can still need a separate fence permit.
  • Front yards are generally limited to 4 feet. Rear yards are generally limited to 6 feet. Corner sight triangles are lower, and a fence has to stay off the public sidewalk.
  • Fences are not allowed in access or drainage easements. Alley setbacks change with how the garage sits. Newer subdivisions add private covenants on top of the city rules.
  • Wind, freeze-thaw, and intense sun are the local wear items: solid panels need real posts, wood needs a finish plan, and steel needs its coating respected at the ground line.
  • Barbed wire and electric fence are not options on a typical residential lot. Barbed wire is limited to employment and industrial zoning.

Questions about fencing in Fort Collins

Do I need a permit for a fence in Fort Collins?

A building permit is not required for a fence 6 feet or shorter, and it is required above that. Front yards are generally capped at 4 feet and rear yards at 6 feet, with tighter limits at corners and along alleys and sidewalks. A fence beside a public right-of-way can still need a city fence permit, and an HOA can be stricter than the city. We check which of those applies before we set a height.

Can you fence a Fort Collins alley lot?

Yes, with the alley setback the city assigns to that garage. Some alley garages need the fence held back several feet so a vehicle can see and swing. Drainage easements are off limits even when the neighbor’s fence is already in one. We lay the line out against those limits instead of copying the fence next door.

Which fence fits a typical city backyard?

A 6-foot wood or vinyl privacy fence is the usual rear-yard screen, with a gate that clears the mower. Chain link is the better fit when the job is a dog yard and privacy is not required. Aluminum is the usual open fence for a front or a pool. The material follows the yard, not a single company default.
